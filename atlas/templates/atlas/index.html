    Built from the novel

    This is a reasoned reconstruction of fictional geography, not a canonical or surveyed map. The supplied English text of IT is the primary source. Film and television geography has not been used.

    The town plan combines both main periods and the historical interludes. Dates in the place cards distinguish former sites and replacements; the map does not depict a single historical day. It covers surface geography only.

    What the symbols mean

    A · Text-anchored relationship. The street, adjoining feature or named site is explicit. The plotted coordinate remains reconstructed.
    B · Inferred position. Routes, proximity and relative directions constrain the placement.
    C · Proposed location. A substantial gap remains; the position is a plausible option.
    U · Unlocated / off map. The place is indexed without an invented coordinate.

    Where the book does not settle the geometry

    The original research notes record the reconstruction decisions and remaining contradictions. Current evidence is shown in the place cards.

    Primary text

    Stephen King, IT. English EPUB supplied by the reader. The exact edition is not reliably identified in its metadata. References name chapters or interludes; P numbers refer to paragraphs in this specific extraction, not printed pages. No complete novel text is included in this website.
